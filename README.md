## 📂 **Featured Projects**

### **🔹 Project 1: Amazon Sales Data Analytics**
**Objective:** Extract actionable insights from multi-year e-commerce sales data  

**Dataset:** ~100K+ transactions | Multiple product categories | Customer demographics  

**Methodology:**
- Loaded raw CSV data into PostgreSQL and performed exploratory analysis
- Cleaned data: handled missing values, standardized formats, detected outliers
- Engineered features: customer lifetime value, category affinity, seasonal factors
- Analyzed revenue drivers, customer segmentation, and product performance

**Key Findings:**
- Identified top 5 revenue-generating product categories contributing 62% of total sales
- Discovered seasonal patterns with Q4 peak of 35% higher transaction volume
- Segmented customers into 4 tiers; high-value segment showed 8x retention advantage
- Recommended inventory optimization strategy based on demand forecasting

**Technical Stack:** Python (Pandas, NumPy), SQL (PostgreSQL), Matplotlib, Seaborn  

**Impact:** Insights directly informed marketing budget allocation and inventory planning  

**Repo:** [Amazon Sales EDA](https://github.com/nehaDataAnalyst/amazon-sales-eda)

---

### **🔹 Project 2: Customer Churn Prediction & Analysis**
**Objective:** Identify at-risk customers and understand churn drivers  

**Dataset:** 10K customer records | Behavioral + transactional data  

**Methodology:**
- Performed cohort analysis and RFM segmentation (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)
- Created churn flags and analyzed behavioral indicators (declining activity, support tickets)
- Built SQL-based dashboards for real-time churn monitoring
- Visualized churn distribution by demographics, product category, tenure

**Key Insights:**
- Customers with <2 support interactions showed 45% higher churn rate
- Month 6-9 post-acquisition is critical; churn peaks at month 8
- Specific product categories had 3x higher churn; recommended intervention

**Technical Stack:** Python (Pandas, Scikit-learn basics), SQL, Excel pivot analysis  

**Repo:** [Customer Churn Analysis](https://github.com/nehaDataAnalyst/churn-analysis)

---

### **🔹 Project 3: Financial KPI Dashboard & Time Series Analysis**
**Objective:** Build automated reporting system for business metrics  

**Dataset:** Monthly revenue, expenses, profit margin data | 5-year historical  

**Methodology:**
- Aggregated data from multiple sources using SQL (UNION, GROUP BY, window functions)
- Performed time series decomposition (trend, seasonality, cyclicity, residuals)
- Created moving averages and calculated YoY growth rates
- Built Excel dashboard with dynamic formulas and sparklines

**Key Deliverables:**
- Automated dashboard updating monthly with zero manual intervention
- Identified profitability crisis in Q3 2023; diagnosed root cause (cost spike)
- Forecasted next 12-month trend with 85% accuracy for planning purposes

**Technical Stack:** SQL (CTEs, window functions), Excel (advanced formulas), Python  

**Repo:** [Financial KPI Dashboard](https://github.com/nehaDataAnalyst/financial-dashboard)

---

### **🔹 Project 4: Market Basket Analysis & Product Recommendations**
**Objective:** Discover product association patterns to optimize cross-selling  

**Dataset:** 50K transactions | Product purchase combinations  

**Methodology:**
- Calculated support, confidence, and lift for product pairs
- Identified strong product associations (e.g., A → B with 70% confidence)
- Segmented insights by customer type and purchase channel
- Visualized association rules in network diagrams

**Key Findings:**
- Product X + Y frequently purchased together (lift = 3.2x random chance)
- Recommended bundle pricing strategy; projected 12% revenue lift
- Top 10 product combinations accounted for 40% of multi-item purchases

**Technical Stack:** Python (Pandas, visualization), SQL queries for aggregation  

**Repo:** [Market Basket Analysis](https://github.com/nehaDataAnalyst/market-basket)
